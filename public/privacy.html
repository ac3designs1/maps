Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 August 2026. Trips is a route planner. This policy covers the iOS app, Android app, and website.

What stays on your phone

Trips, Home/Work pins, and recent places are saved in on-device storage. They are not kept in an account on our servers. Deleting the app removes them.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your current location to start a trip and rank nearby places. Location is used on the device and sent to our server only to look up places and routes you asked for. We do not sell location data.

Search and routes

Addresses and places you search, plus the stops on a route, are sent to our server and to Google Maps Platform (Places, Geocoding, Directions) so we can return results and drive times. Google’s terms also apply to that lookup.

Usage

The app may send anonymous usage pings (for example that a route was planned, device type, and rough screen size) so we can see if the product is working. These are not used to identify you and are not sold.

Calls and other apps

If you tap Waze or Google Maps, your phone opens that app. Those apps have their own privacy policies.
